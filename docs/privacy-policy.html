Privacy Policy — Metronome for Wear OS

Effective date: 2026-05-11  ·  App version: 1.0

Summary

Metronome for Wear OS does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data. The app has no network access and operates entirely on your device.

Data collected

None. The app collects no personal information, usage analytics, crash reports, or any other data that leaves your device.

Local storage

The app saves three preferences to your device's local storage so they are remembered across restarts:

  • Last-used tempo (BPM)
  • Time signature (4/4, 3/4, or 6/8)
  • Feedback mode (audio or vibration)

This data never leaves your device and can be cleared by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

The following system permissions are requested. Each is used solely to operate the metronome and for no other purpose:

  • Vibration — produces haptic beat feedback.
  • Wake Lock — keeps the CPU awake while the metronome is running with the screen off, ensuring accurate timing.
  • Foreground Service — keeps the metronome running reliably in the background while you play an instrument.
  • Post Notifications — shows a persistent notification while the metronome is running so you can stop it from the notification shade.

No permission is used to collect or transmit data of any kind.

Third-party services

The app contains no advertising SDKs, analytics tools, or any other third-party service that could receive data about you or your usage.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the privacy practices of the app change, this page will be updated and the effective date revised. Check this page periodically if you want to stay informed.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the project's GitHub issue tracker.
